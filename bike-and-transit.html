Bikes Public Transit

Bikes can cover the first and last kilometers of a longer trip.

  • A folding bike is the easiest to bring on board.
  • Check each operator's rules for carrying bikes.
  • Travel off-peak when vehicles are less crowded.
  • Park at stations that have secure racks.
Tip: Rules differ by operator, so confirm before your first trip.

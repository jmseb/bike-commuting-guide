Essential Gear

Good gear makes the ride safer, drier and calmer.

Must have

  • Helmet that fits snugly and sits level
  • White front light and red rear light
  • Strong lock (see Locking Parking)
  • Bell or horn
  • Water bottle

Nice to have

  • Fenders for wet roads
  • Rear rack or pannier bag
  • Reflective vest or ankle bands
  • Phone mount
  • Small repair kit
Tip: Turn your lights on when it is cloudy or you ride through shade, not only at night.

Bike Fit Basics

A bike that fits prevents knee, back and wrist pain.

Quick fit check

  1. Seat height - with your heel on the lowest pedal, your leg is almost straight.
  2. Standing over the frame, you have a few centimeters of clearance.
  3. Handlebar reach - elbows stay slightly bent.
  4. Seat is level, not tilted up or down.
  5. Brake levers are easy to reach with your fingers.
Tip: Change one setting at a time and ride a day before changing the next.

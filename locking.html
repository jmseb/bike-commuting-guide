Locking Parking

A good lock and a good spot are your best protection.

Lock it like this

  1. Pick a visible, busy spot.
  2. Lock the frame, and the rear wheel if you can, to a fixed rack or post.
  3. Keep the lock off the ground.
  4. Take lights and bags with you.

Extra protection

  • Photograph the serial number.
  • Use two different types of lock.
  • Avoid leaving the bike out overnight.

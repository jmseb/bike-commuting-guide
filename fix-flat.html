Fixing a Flat Tire

Practice once at home so the roadside fix is quick.

You need

  • Spare inner tube
  • Tire levers
  • Pump

Steps

  1. Move to a safe spot off the road.
  2. Remove the wheel.
  3. Let out the air and pry one side of the tire off with the levers.
  4. Take out the old tube and check inside the tire for thorns or glass.
  5. Put in the new tube with a little air in it.
  6. Refit the tire and wheel.
  7. Inflate to the pressure printed on the tire sidewall.
Tip: Carry a patch kit as a backup in case you get a second flat.

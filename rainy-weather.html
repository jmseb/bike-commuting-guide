Riding in the Rain

Rain is manageable with a little preparation and slower moves.

Before you ride

  • Fit fenders if you can.
  • Wear a waterproof jacket and cover your bag.
  • Check that your brakes grip well.

On wet roads

  • Brake earlier and more gently.
  • Avoid painted lines and metal covers, which get slippery.
  • Watch for puddles that hide potholes.

After the ride

  1. Wipe down the frame.
  2. Dry and lightly oil the chain.
  3. Hang your gear to dry.
Tip: Skip the ride during typhoon warnings or flooding and take public transit instead.

Road Safety Rules

Most close calls come from surprises. Make your moves easy to read.

Ride predictably

  • Ride with traffic, never against it.
  • Stop at red lights and stop signs.
  • Keep about one meter from parked cars to avoid opening doors.
  • Look behind before changing lanes.
  • Skip the phone and earphones while riding.

Be seen

  • Use lights at dusk and in rain.
  • Wear bright or reflective clothing.
  • Make eye contact with drivers at junctions.
Tip: Follow your local traffic rules and city or municipal ordinances.

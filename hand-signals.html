Hand Signals

Signals let drivers and other riders know your next move.

Left turn
Extend your left arm straight out.
Right turn
Extend your right arm straight out.
Slowing or stopping
Extend your left arm down, palm facing back.
Hazard ahead
Point to the hazard so riders behind can avoid it.
Tip: Signal early, then put both hands back on the bars before you turn.

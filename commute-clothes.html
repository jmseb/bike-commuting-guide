What to Wear?

You do not need special clothes. You need smart choices.

Clothing Tips

  • Wear layers you can remove.
  • Choose breathable fabric.
  • Strap or roll up pant legs so they stay off the chain.
  • Wear closed shoes with grip.

Arriving fresh

  1. Ride at an easy pace.
  2. Keep a spare shirt in your bag.
  3. Use wipes and deodorant.
  4. Roll clothes to keep them from wrinkling.

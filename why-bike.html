Why Bike to Work or School?

For trips under about 8 km, a bike is often as fast as a ride stuck in traffic, and it costs almost nothing.

What you gain?

  • Lower daily cost - no fare, no fuel, no parking fee.
  • Predictable travel time - you pass traffic instead of waiting in it.
  • Exercise built into your day.
  • Less noise and cleaner air on your street.

Sample weekly cost comparison

Mode Weekly Cost Notes
Bike ₱0–₱100 Occasional repairs
Jeepney or bus ₱400–₱800 Depends on route
Motorcycle ₱500–₱900 Fuel and parking

These are sample estimates only. Replace them with your own fares and fuel costs.

Tip: Start with one or two days a week. Build the habit before you go all in.

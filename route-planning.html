Planning Your Route

The shortest route is not always the safest one.

Steps

  1. Check a map app's cycling layer.
  2. Prefer calm streets, bike lanes and park paths.
  3. Avoid fast roads with no shoulder.
  4. Do a weekend test ride at your usual commute time.
  5. Save a backup route for rain or road work.

Questions to ask

  • Where are the steep hills?
  • Which intersections feel risky?
  • Where can I rest or refill water?
Tip: Ride the route once as a practice run before your first real commute.

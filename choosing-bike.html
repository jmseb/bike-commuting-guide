Choosing a Bike

The best commuter bike is the one you will actually ride every day.

Common types

These are the most common bikes for commuting.

Hybrid

Upright seat and smooth tires. Comfortable on city roads.

Mountain Bike

Sturdy and easy to find. Swap to smoother tires for pavement.

Road Bike

Fast and light, but the posture is more bent over.

Folding Bike

Fits on buses and trains, and under a desk.

Electric-Assist

Helps with hills, heat and long distances.

Before you buy

  1. Set a budget that also covers a lock, lights and a helmet.
  2. Test ride at least two bikes.
  3. Check that it can take a rear rack and fenders.
  4. Ask the shop about warranty and repairs.
Tip: A reliable secondhand bike from a trusted shop is a good way to start.
